## About Twiga Brew Café
<!-- keywords: kuhusu sisi, mkahawa, kahawa, hadithi, ni nini -->
Twiga Brew Café is a *fictional demo business* created to show the Jibu chatbot. Imagine a friendly specialty coffee café in Westlands, Nairobi, serving Kenyan-grown coffee, all-day breakfast, light lunches and fresh juices. We roast beans from Nyeri and Kiambu farms and bake on site every morning. Everyone is welcome: remote workers, families and groups of friends.

## Opening hours and public holidays

Our opening times: we are open every day of the week.
- Monday to Friday: opens at 7:00, closes at 21:00
- Saturday: opens at 8:00, closes at 22:00
- Sunday: opens at 8:00, closes at 20:00

The kitchen stops taking food orders 45 minutes before closing.

Public holidays: on public holidays we open from 9:00 to 18:00, even when the holiday falls on a Sunday. On Christmas Day (25 December) we are closed. On New Year's Day (1 January) we open at 12:00 and close at 18:00.

## Dietary needs and allergens
<!-- keywords: mboga mboga, bila nyama, mzio, vegan, mlo, chakula, halal, bila gluteni -->
- Vegetarian: breakfast dishes, pilau, salad, pancakes and the sandwich without chicken.
- Vegan: chapati with dengu stew, avocado toast without egg, garden salad without feta, all juices and drinks made with oat or almond milk.
- Gluten: our kitchen handles wheat, so we cannot promise gluten-free meals. Eggs, salad, juices and coffee are made without gluten ingredients.
- Halal: our chicken and beef are halal.
- Allergens: we handle nuts, milk, eggs, wheat, soy and sesame. Almond milk and some desserts may contain nuts. Please tell the staff about any allergy when you order. We cannot guarantee a nut-free kitchen.

## Delivery zones and fees
<!-- keywords: usafirishaji, kuleta, tunaleta, bei ya kusafirisha, eneo, Kilimani, Karen, Lavington -->
We deliver by motorbike rider inside these Nairobi zones. The minimum delivery order is KES 500.
- Zone 1 (Westlands, Parklands, Lavington): KES 150, about 25 minutes
- Zone 2 (Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lower Kabete, Gigiri): KES 250, about 40 minutes
- Zone 3 (Karen, Runda, Kitisuru, Ngong Road): KES 400, about 60 minutes

Delivery in Zone 1 is free for orders above KES 2,000. We do not deliver outside these zones. Delivery orders are taken until 19:30 every day. Heavy rain can slow riders down.

You can order at the counter, from your table, or for delivery. We accept M-Pesa, Visa and Mastercard cards, and cash. Prices include 16% VAT and there is no service charge.

Paying with M-Pesa: choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Buy Goods and Services, and enter Till 000000 (demo). This till number is fake and only for this demo. Send the exact amount and show the confirmation SMS to the cashier. For delivery you can pay by M-Pesa before dispatch, or pay cash to the rider on orders up to KES 5,000.

## Wi-Fi and working from the café

Free Wi-Fi is available for all customers at about 50 Mbps. The network is called TwigaBrew-Guest and the password is printed on your receipt. Most window and wall tables have power sockets. We ask that you order a drink or snack for every 2 hours you stay.

## Loyalty card
<!-- keywords: kadi ya uaminifu, pointi, mihuri, zawadi, punguzo, bure -->
Join the free Twiga Card by scanning the QR code at the counter. You earn 1 stamp for every KES 500 you spend, on dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders. Collect 10 stamps to get a free drink worth up to KES 400. Stamps expire after 6 months. During your birthday week you get a free slice of cake.

## Refunds and complaints
<!-- keywords: kurudishiwa pesa, malalamiko, tatizo, kosa, rudisha, lalamika, fidia -->
If something is wrong with your food or drink, tell us within 30 minutes (dine-in) or within 2 hours (delivery) and we will remake it or refund you. M-Pesa refunds arrive within 24 hours and card refunds within 3 to 5 working days. If a delivery is more than 30 minutes later than promised, we refund the delivery fee. You can cancel an order for free before the kitchen starts preparing it. For complaints, ask for the duty manager. We reply to written complaints within 2 working days.
